V3 frozen figure/table inputs

Nine byte-identical completed-result inputs and the archived Figure1 image are
listed in provenance.json. Their original source paths are provenance only.
V3 build_figures.py requires the bundled copies and never falls back to a
mutable repository. Missing or changed files stop the analysis.

The default notebook verifies these hashes, redraws Figures2–4 into
outputs/reproduced_figures and reuses Figure1 unchanged. Mean paired effects
are reconstructed from counts; original95% and97.5% confidence endpoints and
r90 time means are retained, not regenerated by resampling/raw trajectories.

See README_ANALYSIS.txt for the exact default entry point, environment and
fresh-kernel execution receipt. The older portability_validation.json records
the previous unchanged-data figure design test; current execution is recorded
in outputs/notebook_execution_verification.json.
