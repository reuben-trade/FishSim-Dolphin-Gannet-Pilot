fig1_model: Model rules and one recorded encounter. (a) Both dolphin target policies use the same 2T near-prey herding cycle, one feasible strike and normal handling/recovery. Gannets influence dolphins through prey dynamics, without a dolphin response to a bird cue. (b,c) Actual top and side views at 12T for development school 39061002, exposed targeting with gannets; lines show predator paths over 6–12T and crosses mark actual captures in that interval. Horizontal coordinates are relative to the surviving fish centroid at 12T; z=0 is the surface. Coordinates are model units. This snapshot illustrates the model, not successful whole-school containment.

fig2_development: Development comparison of the two dolphin target-selection rules. Bars show actual dolphin captures in matched 36T encounters with 12 dolphins, with or without 3 gannets. Each policy was tested on the same two development schools with one random-stream setting. Nearest targeting changed from 55 to 53 captures in total; exposed targeting changed from 41 to 48. The exposed policy was selected for follow-up on this development evidence. Individual school outcomes are shown without confidence intervals.

fig3_replication: Partner effects and numerical sensitivity on the same eight schools. Points show mixed-minus-alone captures; lines join each school across coarse (0.00125T) and fine (0.000625T) grids at 36T. Diamonds show equal-school means. Only the coarse 36T primary estimates have 97.5% marginal bootstrap intervals (approximate 95% familywise coverage, with small-sample limitations). Fine 36T and coarse 72T results are descriptive sensitivity and duration checks, not additional independent schools. Earlier cohorts are excluded.

fig4_mechanism: Matching gannet-only controls and initial-depth results. (a) Dolphin capture change (DG minus D) and gannet change (DG minus G) for the same four fresh-school comparisons. S1=39063501 and S2=39063502; repetitions 1 and 2 identify settings 61001 and 61002. Both species improve in two repetitions; four comparisons represent two independent schools. (b) Gannet-only captures over 36T across initial vertical translations, using setting 61001; lines join the same school, not fitted response curves. (c) Fraction of surviving fish within the reference target-depth envelope 7.93028, with colour identifying initial mean depth and solid/dashed lines identifying schools 1/2. This is a geometric eligibility reference, not guaranteed interception. Initial depth is not maintained; movement and selective removal can alter the survivor fraction. No uncertainty intervals are shown.
