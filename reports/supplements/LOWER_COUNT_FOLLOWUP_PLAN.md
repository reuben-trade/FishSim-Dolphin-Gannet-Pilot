# Prospective addendum: H2/G12 follow-up and four-endpoint programme

**Planned, not authorised for native execution.** This dated public addendum is posted after the H1/G36 primary study began, but before any H2/G12 feeding. It is not formal preregistration. The H2/G12 target was already named in [the original root design](../../prospective_plans/independent_validation_2026-11-10/ROOT_DESIGN_AUTHORIZATION.json) before primary feeding. Its detailed secondary analysis and additional programme family are declared later, before the first complete paired coarse/fine block or fine-grid feeding-effect analysis. [Posting context](../../prospective_plans/independent_lower_count_validation_2026-11-10/POSTING_CONTEXT.json) distinguishes these events; the original H1/G36 plan is unchanged.

## Same cohort, new matched encounters

The follow-up uses the **same 16 schools** (39065501–39065516), the same archived 20T states and labels 71001/71002. The labels are nested stochastic-world repeats, not new schools or solely capture-probability noise. Across both settings there are still **16 school clusters and 32 labelled worlds**, not 32 independent schools.

H2/G12 uses twelve dolphins, twelve gannets, herding minimum 2T, fish 160 and unchanged q=0.7 eligible-contact capture. It needs **new H2 dolphin-only, G12-only and H2/G12 mixed encounters**. H1 dolphin-only or G36-only controls cannot substitute. Coarse dt 0.00125T and fine dt 0.000625T each last a nominal 36T; coherent early depletion retains that denominator. Physical rules, controller logic, stable-ID readiness/private-stream formulas and point-body assumptions remain unchanged.

The [192 planned identities](../../prospective_plans/independent_lower_count_validation_2026-11-10/case_inventory.csv) were generated purely from the exact archived [case source](../../prospective_plans/independent_lower_count_validation_2026-11-10/cases.py). This adds 96 cases per grid and 64 per arm, with no new predator-free preparation. The full proposed ceiling is 8,294,400 steps plus 28,800 for six separate 4T engineering cases: **8,323,200 planned steps, none authorised by this preparation**. Both studies together would have 384 full encounters on the same 16 schools. [Local inventory checks](../../prospective_plans/independent_lower_count_validation_2026-11-10/README.md) load no feeding data or model.

## Secondary estimation, without a second success decision

For each species, form integer mixed-minus-matched-control capture differences, divide by 12 individuals and average both labels within each school, then average sixteen school effects. Units are **fish captured per individual over 36T**. Rates divide by 36 separately. All label/school effects, signs, absolute/group returns, school SD/SE and conditional repeat MCSE remain visible; repeat noise is not added twice to the school SE.

The fine-grid follow-up uses **95% pointwise school-mean Student-t intervals**, df 15, critical 2.131449545559776. These are secondary estimation intervals, **not a new confirmatory pass/fail family**; the automated secondary decision is null. The fixed 50,000-draw whole-school-vector bootstrap (PCG64 seed 8611030; quantiles 0.025/0.975) is sensitivity only. Coarse and fine-minus-coarse contrasts remain secondary. All settings/labels/species/grids remain paired when resampled.

The original H1/G36 **97.5% primary intervals and two-species criterion stay unchanged**. H2/G12 cannot rescue, replace or revise that decision. Report both estimates and intervals, including unfavourable findings; do not choose an interval method according to which passes.

## Additional four-endpoint programme family

After **both complete 192-case studies pass independent final audits**, the following four fine-grid endpoints will always be analysed together, irrespective of their signs:

| Endpoint | Setting | Species |
|---|---|---|
| fine_H1_G36_D | H1/G36 | Dolphin partner gain |
| fine_H1_G36_G | H1/G36 | Gannet partner gain |
| fine_H2_G12_D | H2/G12 | Dolphin partner gain |
| fine_H2_G12_G | H2/G12 | Gannet partner gain |

The additional analysis uses **98.75% two-sided school-level t intervals**, df 15, critical 2.8366274760941357, targeting approximate 95% simultaneous Bonferroni coverage under the individual interval assumptions. A programme-wide claim that both settings benefit both species requires **all four lower bounds above zero**. This calculation is predeclared for execution after both audits, not selected after seeing favourable results. The original primary and secondary outputs are retained alongside it.

School vectors stay paired across both settings and labels: n remains 16. H1/G36 versus H2/G12 changes **both herding duration and bird number**, so it cannot isolate either factor or establish an optimum. Encounter-grid comparisons remain conditional on the same coarse-prepared starts, not preparation convergence. Statistical criteria do not establish biological generalisation or energetic profitability.
