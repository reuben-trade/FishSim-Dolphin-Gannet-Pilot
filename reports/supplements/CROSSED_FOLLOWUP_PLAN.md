# Prospective crossed follow-up: herding duration × bird count

**Design accepted for public archiving; native execution is not authorised.** This adds the two missing mixed settings, H1/G12 and H2/G36, to the prospective H1/G36 and H2/G12 studies. It was designed after primary control runs began and before the first complete paired feeding effects were inspected. This later public posting is **not formal preregistration**. The [posting context](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/POSTING_CONTEXT.json), [independent design review](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/INDEPENDENT_DESIGN_REVIEW.json) and [root acceptance](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/ROOT_DESIGN_ACCEPTANCE.json) record that chronology and limited authority. No validation feeding effects are reported here.

## Complete the crossed design on the same schools

| Herding minimum | 12 gannets | 36 gannets |
|---|---|---|
| H1 = 1T | New proposed mixed cases | Original active primary study |
| H2 = 2T | Previously planned follow-up | New proposed mixed cases |

H is the continuous local prey-proximity time, within 4.5 model distance, required before an eligible dolphin strike under the shared rule. It is a controller timing threshold, rather than a measured completion of school compaction.

The design uses the **same 16 schools** (39065501–39065516), labels 71001/71002, twelve dolphins and 160 initial fish. Each setting has both coarse dt 0.00125T and fine dt 0.000625T encounters lasting a nominal 36T. The two labels are nested stochastic-world repeats, not additional schools. There are 32 labelled worlds and 64 school/label/grid combinations; inference still uses **16 school clusters**.

The [128 new mixed identities](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/new_mixed_cases.csv) are 16 schools × 2 labels × 2 grids × 2 new settings. They reuse exact H1/H2 dolphin-only and G12/G36 bird-only controls from the two 192-case studies. The [case-control map](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/new_case_control_map.csv) and [paired map](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/paired_control_map.csv) identify those references explicitly. Control references are not new executions. After every planned stage completes, the bank would contain **512 unique full encounters**, including 256 unique controls, on these same 16 schools—not n = 512 independent replicates.

There is no additional school preparation or change to the physical engine, contact rule, dolphin policy logic or private-stream formulas. Eligible oral-contact success remains q = 0.7. Bird identities are nested at the original three fixed launch sites: four birds per site at G12 and twelve at G36. Changing bird count therefore changes local multiplicity in this point-body layout; it does not introduce a collision, avoidance or separate spatial-coverage model.

## Estimate both factors and their interaction

For each labelled world, dolphin partner gain is mixed minus same-H dolphin-only captures, divided by 12. Bird partner gain is mixed minus same-count bird-only captures, divided by 12 or 36 birds. Units are **fish per individual over nominal 36T**. Average both labels within each school before calculating uncertainty; time rates divide by 36 separately.

For each species, the [analysis plan](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/analysis_plan.json) specifies four cell effects, two bird-count contrasts, two herding-duration contrasts and one interaction: nine components per species, or an **18-component paired school vector**. Count contrasts compare G36 minus G12 at each H; herding contrasts compare H2 minus H1 at each count. The interaction is E(2,36) − E(2,12) − E(1,36) + E(1,12). Integer numerators preserve exact control cancellations and zeros before division.

Fine-grid results are **secondary estimation with 95% pointwise school-level t intervals**, df 15, critical 2.131449545559776. Report all school/world points, signs, mean, sample SD and SE. These intervals provide no simultaneous family coverage, and this plan defines **no new automated success decision or bootstrap**. Coarse estimates and paired fine-minus-coarse differences remain sensitivity analyses.

The original H1/G36 **97.5% primary analysis** and the additional four-endpoint **98.75% programme analysis** remain unchanged. New settings cannot rescue or replace either. Raw group food and absolute per-animal return remain separate from partner gain. The crossed design estimates H and bird-count contrasts at these two tested levels, conditional on this model and cohort; it does not establish an optimum or biological generality.

## Complete-data and execution gates

Both 192-case cohorts must first finish and pass **independent FINAL evidence audits, irrespective of effect signs**. Future reuse must bind exact source execution, completion and final-review hashes. Empty completion fields in the prospective maps are deliberately pending; they must not be fabricated, aliased or filled with zeros.

Only then may fresh finite root authority and reviewed executable source, input, actual-start, engineering and feeding gates permit the 128 new cases. All 128 remain mandatory regardless of findings. The [four proposed 4T engineering cases](../../prospective_plans/crossed_herding_gannet_followup_2026-11-10/engineering_smokes.csv) are excluded from feeding estimates. Planned work is 5,529,600 full-run steps plus 19,200 engineering steps: **5,548,800 proposed steps, none authorised by this design archive**. No native execution harness is implemented here.
