# Higher-count checkpoint: 48 gannets, coarse grid

**In this completed coarse-grid phase on four known schools, adding 48 gannets accompanies positive dolphin and bird school-mean gains in all four schools.** The matched 36-gannet comparison has joint gains in two of four. These are early descriptive results; the fine-grid and 72-gannet cases remain mandatory and pending here.

Twelve dolphins use minimum herding of 1T, with 160 starting fish and unchanged conditional contact success q=0.7. Each known school has two nested randomness labels, averaged before the four-school mean. The grid is 0.00125T and food is measured over the planned 36T encounter. This snapshot contains **16 new cases plus 24 matching coarse-grid primary references: 40 unique encounters**. Eight labelled encounters contribute to each arm total; the independent school count is four, not eight worlds or forty executions.

Mean gains are fish per individual over planned 36T. Percentages are ratios of matched eight-world capture totals, not averages of per-school percentages. Bird-only controls match bird count; the dolphin-only control is shared by these two comparisons.

Mixed food per dolphin rises from 2.1042 with 36 birds to 2.2708 with 48. **Mixed food per bird also rises here, from 0.3611 to 0.4141**; it does not always decline when more birds are added. The matched comparison uses these same four schools, labels and coarse grid. The paper's full sixteen-school fine-grid primary estimate is a different summary and remains unchanged.
