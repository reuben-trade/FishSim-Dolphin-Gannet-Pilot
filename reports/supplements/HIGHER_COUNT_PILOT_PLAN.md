# Prospective higher-count pilot: 48 and 72 gannets

This exploratory plan was fixed after the completed primary results and the first eight-school H2/G12 results were known, but before any higher-count input assembly, constructors or feeding. It is prospective documentation, not formal preregistration. It adds outcomes on four already-observed schools, rather than new independent schools.

## Fixed comparison

The pilot keeps twelve dolphins, minimum herding duration H=1T, 160 initial fish and the unchanged conditional oral-contact capture probability q=0.7. It compares **48 and 72 gannets** with the existing 36-gannet reference. Encounters cover nominal 36T on both grids: coarse 0.00125T and fine 0.000625T. T is uncalibrated model time. Both grids reuse the same accepted 20T prepared checkpoint; no new preparations or replacement schools are planned.

The four fixed primary-study schools are **39065501–39065504**, each with labels **71001 and 71002**. Labels are averaged within each school before the four schools receive equal weight; eight labelled worlds are not eight independent schools.

There are **64 new full encounters**: four schools × two labels × two grids × two bird counts × bird-only/mixed groups. The **48 retained 36-bird reference encounters already include the 16 dolphin-only controls**. The complete comparison therefore has **112 unique full encounters**, not 128. Dolphin-only controls are reused; each new bird count gets its own bird-only controls.

## Order and reporting

The fixed phase order is **48 coarse → 48 fine → 72 coarse → 72 fine**. Each phase completes eight bird-only controls before eight mixed encounters. **All 64 new cases are mandatory regardless of signs.** No favourable-result stopping, replacement or additional count search is planned.

After both 48-bird grids are complete and independently audited, a descriptive checkpoint may report their 32 new cases together with the 48 references: **80 unique encounters, n=4 schools**. Every 72-bird case still proceeds. Both grids and all gains, ties and losses will be retained.

Reports will separate group catches from captures per dolphin and per bird, including species-matched partner differences. Food uses the planned 36T denominator even if complete prey depletion ends physical integration early. No confidence intervals, p-values, bootstrap, automated success criterion or optimal-group claim is planned for this pilot.

## Scheduling and limits

To examine larger bird groups sooner, the prospective queue is: **complete H2/G12 → higher-count pilot → fixed crossed follow-up → fresh sixteen-school replication → D24 sensitivity**. H2/G12 must finish all 192 cases and its independent integrity audit; favourable effects are not a prerequisite. The other studies retain their fixed designs and remain queued regardless of pilot outcomes.

Birds share the same three initial sites: sixteen point birds per site at G48 and twenty-four at G72. There is no added spatial coverage or predator–predator collision/avoidance model. This abundance test cannot identify realistic competition, coordination or synchronisation mechanisms.

Execution remains closed pending the preceding study and new source, start-state, engineering and independent-audit checks. The finite ceiling includes 64 zero-step starts, eight fixed 4T engineering cases and 64 full cases: **136 initializations and 2,803,200 model steps**, with no extra benchmarks. The current paper, main notebook and four figures remain unchanged.
